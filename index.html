Geography

Seven regions, four seas

Türkiye is bordered by the Black Sea, the Aegean, the Mediterranean, and surrounds the Sea of Marmara. Geographers divide it into seven regions. Click a region on the map, or a name below it, to explore it. Hover over the map to see each province.

History
