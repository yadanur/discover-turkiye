Privacy Policy

Discover Türkiye app and website · Last updated 31 October 2026

Discover Türkiye is an educational guide to Türkiye. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, store or sell any personal information.

Location

Your location is used only when you tap Find place to order on a food. The app asks for permission first, and you can say no. If you allow it, the app rounds your position to about 1 km and sends only that rough area to Photon, a public search service for OpenStreetMap data, to find nearby restaurants. Your location is not saved by the app or by us, and it is never linked to who you are.

Content from other services

  • Videos open on YouTube, and other links open in an in-app browser. Those sites follow their own privacy policies.
  • The page's fonts load from Google Fonts when you are online.

Children

The app is suitable for students and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the project page: github.com/yadanur/discover-turkiye/issues.
